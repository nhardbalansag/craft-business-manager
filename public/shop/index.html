A FEW LOVELY ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Need something else? Send us a message and we'll be glad to discuss it.

Can I request customized colors or designs?

Yes, you can send your preferences through our Facebook page. We will confirm what designs and customizations are possible for your request.

How can I check the price of a product?

Message us with the collection or product you're interested in and the quantity you need. We'll confirm the current price and availability.

Do you accept event or bulk inquiries?

You can inquire about orders for events or multiple pieces. Include your target quantity, occasion and date so we can discuss the details.

Where can I place an order?

Contact My Lovely Craft through the official Facebook page linked throughout this website. This page is a product showcase, not an online checkout.
