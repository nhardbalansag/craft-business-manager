Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the My Lovely Craft Android app (package com.mylovelycraft.mobile) handles information while supporting product catalogs, production planning, sales quotations, point-of-sale receipts, product labels and Bluetooth printing.

App: My Lovely Craft Operator: My Lovely Craft Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Scope and how the app works

The app is a workshop and point-of-sale companion for My Lovely Craft. Its catalog is obtained from a Google Sheets workbook published by the business. The app saves a working copy of the catalog and operational records on the Android device so key features can work offline. Some features use a paired Bluetooth printer or the Android print/PDF workflow when you choose to print or export.

This policy is for the My Lovely Craft mobile app. The related Craft Business Manager website hosts this policy and provides separate browser-based business tools; your use of any separate website or service may be subject to that service's own data-handling arrangements.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features used, the app can access and store the following information:

  • Business catalog and production information: product names, identifiers, categories, photos, stock and material details, recipes, pricing and financial cost records, suppliers, production requests and quotation details from the published workbook or entered in the app.
  • Customer and sales information: customer names and, if entered, contact details and addresses; quoted or purchased items, quantities, selling prices, discounts, transaction references, payment method, amount received and balance, notes, saved quotations and receipts.
  • Printer and device configuration: paired Bluetooth printer names/addresses, printer settings, print jobs and status required for printing. Android controls permissions for nearby/Bluetooth devices.
  • Locally created documents: product tags, production printouts, customer receipts and quotation PDFs that you choose to print, save or share.
  • Support messages: information you decide to send when contacting My Lovely Craft for help or a privacy request through the contact channel below.

The current Android app does not offer in-app account registration. It records payment methods and amounts for receipts; it is not a payment card processing service. Do not enter passwords, full payment card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information in free-text notes.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to display the published product catalog, plan production and material needs, calculate business prices and quotations, prepare and reprint receipts, remember user-selected settings, generate product identifiers and labels, send user-requested print jobs, restore saved work offline, and respond to support or privacy inquiries.

The app uses a network connection to download or refresh the business's published Google Sheets workbook. It does not automatically send locally saved customer receipts or quotations to a My Lovely Craft server as part of the current documented mobile workflow.

4. Sharing and third-party services

My Lovely Craft does not sell customer information. Information may be transferred or become accessible to the following types of parties only in connection with the described features or your choices:

  • Google (Google Sheets): when the app downloads a workbook published to the web, Google receives the request and processes network information under its own privacy practices. Information the publisher places in a publicly published workbook may be accessible to others with that published link.
  • Your selected Bluetooth printer and its manufacturer: when you choose to print, receipt or label content is sent to the paired device over Bluetooth; physical printouts are visible to people who handle them.
  • Android print services and user-selected recipients: when you choose to save a PDF, print through Android or share an exported document, the selected service, storage destination or recipient may receive the document contents.
  • Facebook (Meta): if you contact us through our Facebook page, your message and profile information are handled through that platform under its privacy terms.

Hosting providers may also process standard connection information when you visit this public policy page or the separate website. The Android app's published-workbook network requests and optional document exports are distinct from automatic upload of your locally saved sales records. The reviewed Android app dependencies do not show an integrated advertising or analytics SDK; this policy will be updated if the released app's data practices change.

5. Device permissions and access

The app uses Internet access for workbook refreshes and Bluetooth or Nearby devices access to discover and connect to an Android-paired printer when you use printing features. Permissions are governed by Android and can be managed in your device settings. Product photos downloaded with the workbook are kept in app-managed device storage for offline display.

6. Security

Operational records are stored in the app's local Android storage. The current Android build disables automatic Android backup for the app and disallows cleartext network traffic; Google Sheets workbook downloads use HTTPS. Bluetooth printing occurs only when requested by a user and requires the applicable Android permission. However, no application or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your unlocked device, printed receipts and exported files, particularly when they contain customer contact or financial information.

Access to copies of customer data saved or shared outside the app (such as printed paper, exported PDFs, published Google Sheets, or third-party messaging services) is governed by the relevant destination and your own access controls.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

The app retains the imported workbook snapshot, downloaded product photos, selected printer settings, and locally saved operational records while the app remains installed and those records are in use. The current saved state keeps up to the most recent 100 records in each of these categories: production/order requests, print jobs, quotations and customer receipts. Older entries in those categories are dropped from the saved app history as newer ones are retained; no specific automatic time-based deletion is promised.

You can erase locally stored app data through Android Settings → Apps → My Lovely Craft → Storage → Clear storage (exact menu labels may vary), or by uninstalling the app. Clearing app storage also removes offline catalog data and saved app history. PDFs saved to other folders, information in published Google Sheets, external backup copies and printed receipts must be removed separately from their respective locations.

The current app does not provide personal user accounts. For requests about personal information you have shared directly with My Lovely Craft through support, including access, correction or deletion, use the contact mechanism below. We will review and respond to requests as applicable under relevant law; records that must be kept for legal or accounting reasons may be retained for the legally required period.

8. Children's privacy

This app is a business management and point-of-sale tool intended for business operators, not a service directed at children. Craft products sold by the business may be intended for children, but that does not make the business administration app a children's app. Do not enter a child's personal information into customer records unless it is necessary and lawfully obtained.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as app features, service providers or data practices change. The updated text and revision date will appear on this page. Material changes may require additional in-app disclosure or consent where applicable.
